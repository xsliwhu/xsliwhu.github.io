About

I am a Professor in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at Wuhan University. My research interests lie in several complex variables, CR geometry, and complex geometry.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Wuhan University in 2013 under the supervision of Professor Xiaojun Huang, and was a visiting student at Rutgers University from 2011 to 2013.
